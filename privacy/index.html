Site notice effective October 28, 2026. This notice describes the features of this website concept. The school must verify its wider business practices and any hosting, email, or other service-provider policies before a public launch.

Who to contact

Wings Over L.A. Aviation, LLC, 7900 Balboa Blvd., Suite J1, Van Nuys, CA 91406. For privacy questions or requests, email info@wingsoverla.net or call (818) 922-2449.

Notice at collection: inquiry details

The inquiry tool lets you enter a name, email, optional phone number, training interest, current certificate or rating, preferred timing, an optional message, and your optional marketing preference. It uses those entries only to prepare an email on your device. They are not submitted to a website database, retained in browser storage, or sent to the school by this page. Your email provider processes the message if you choose to send it.

If you send an inquiry, the school receives the information to respond, discuss training, and arrange requested services. Do not include medical details, passport numbers, payment information, or other sensitive documents in the form.

Optional marketing messages

The second inquiry checkbox is optional and starts unchecked. If you select it, your email draft records your choice, the consent wording, its version, and when the draft was prepared. Email is included; text messages are included only if you provide a phone number. You can request a response to your inquiry without agreeing to marketing, and marketing consent is not a condition of purchase. If you send the email with this permission, the school receives your preference along with your inquiry. This preview does not automatically enroll you in a mailing list or send marketing messages. To withdraw permission, contact info@wingsoverla.net, use the unsubscribe option in a marketing email, or reply STOP to a marketing text.

Browser storage & tracking

This site includes no advertising pixels, behavioral advertising, or third-party analytics. The chat greeting timer and dismissal are kept only in this page’s memory; no browser storage is used for them. Older versions may have left a chat greeting preference in session storage, which this version does not read. Fonts, images, and videos are served with the site. The former automatic privacy notice has been removed; older versions may have left a privacy acknowledgement in local storage, which you can clear through your browser.

Hosting and authentication providers may process technical request data such as IP address, browser information, request time, and security or login information to deliver and protect the site. The private concept is hosted through Sites and may require sign-in. Provider processing and retention follow their own policies and must be reviewed before public launch.

AI chat preview

The pre-launch Wings AI Assistant simulates replies to three sample flight training questions. It does not accept typed messages or connect to a live AI model or SDK in this preview. Your selected questions and the displayed replies remain in this page while it is open. The site does not store chat history or send these selections to the school. A small question bubble appears 10 seconds after the page finishes loading. The chat window opens only when you click the bubble or chat button. Opening or dismissing the greeting keeps it closed until you load another page or reload this one. The green dot indicates that the assistant preview is available; it does not indicate a connected live AI service. The question bubble waits while a form field is focused or the browser tab is hidden. If you follow a contact, email, or phone link, those services work as described elsewhere in this notice.

Sale, sharing & browser signals

This website does not include tools that sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. No optional tracking loads, whether or not you enable Global Privacy Control (GPC) or Do Not Track. The privacy preferences panel reports whether your browser sends GPC. There is no sale or behavioral advertising setting to opt into.

Your choices & requests

You can use this site without completing the inquiry tool, leave optional fields blank, call instead of emailing, and clear local storage in your browser. To ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy choices concerning an inquiry sent to the school, use the contact details above. The school may need information to verify and fulfill a request. California statutory rights depend on the applicable law and the business’s coverage; this notice does not determine that coverage.

Retention & service providers

Unsaved inquiry entries remain only in the page while it is open; browser autofill or email draft behavior is controlled by your own browser and email provider. Sent inquiries become part of the school’s correspondence. The school must confirm its retention periods, deletion practices, and any service-provider disclosures before using this concept as its public privacy policy.

External links & children

Phone, email, and map links open services you choose to use. Those services have their own privacy practices. This site is not directed to children under 13. Please contact the school with a parent or guardian for questions about youth flight experiences.

Changes to this notice

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Questions or concerns may be sent to the school using the contact information above.
