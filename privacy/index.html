This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website, contact us, book a flight lesson or air tour, or sign up to receive text messages from us.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, mobile phone number, preferred dates, the service you're interested in (flight training or air tours), and any message you send us.
  • Booking and service information: lesson and tour bookings, scheduling details, and payment records handled by our payment processor.
  • Automatically collected information: basic website usage data such as browser type, pages visited, and approximate location, collected through cookies and similar tools.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To schedule and confirm flight lessons, discovery flights, and air tours, and to send reminders.
  • To send you text messages you have agreed to receive (see Section 4).
  • To send you information about our services and offers, if you opt in.
  • To improve our website and services, and to meet our legal obligations.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us run our business (for example, our scheduling, CRM, messaging, email, website hosting, and payment providers), only as needed to provide those services and under obligations to protect it. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

4. Text messaging (SMS) and mobile information

  • If you opt in to text messages from Wings Over L.A., we use your mobile number to send messages about your enquiry, lesson and tour bookings, reminders, customer care, and, if you agree, occasional offers.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at (818) 922-2449 or info@wingsoverla.net.
  • We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise provide mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Mobile information may be shared only with service providers that help us deliver text messages (such as our messaging platform and carriers), solely to provide the messaging service. All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

5. Data retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect it.

6. Your choices and California privacy rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and you can opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link and out of texts by replying STOP. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), including the right to know, delete, and correct personal information and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To make a request, email info@wingsoverla.net or call (818) 922-2449.

7. Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 online. Minors interested in flight training should contact us with a parent or guardian.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
