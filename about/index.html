MEET THE OWNER

Built by a pilot.
For people who
want to fly.

Jess Fernandez founded Wings Over L.A. Aviation in 2018. As Owner and Chief Pilot, he holds an ATP (Airline Transport Pilot) certificate and has 8,000 flight hours. He brings a longstanding connection to aviation in the Los Angeles area.

The school brings together instructors and a single- and multi-engine fleet at Van Nuys Airport. Start with a visit, a conversation, or an introductory flight.

How we train at Van Nuys

Mission and vision

Help people build confidence and sound judgment in the cockpit, from their first flight to advanced training.

Commitment to safety

Wings Over L.A. emphasizes aircraft maintenance and safety throughout flight training. Talk with the team about preflight planning, operating practices and instructor support.

Training approach

The academy uses the Cessna Flight Training system to connect ground study, flight lessons and syllabus-based progress tracking.

Academy facilities

Visit the academy at Prop Park VNY, 7900 Balboa Blvd., Suite J1, Van Nuys, CA 91406. Ask the team about the hangars, ground-training facilities and simulator.
