Find your training program

Private Pilot Certificate

Course overview
Build a foundation in single-engine flying through ground study and instructor-led flights.
What you’ll learn
Preflight planning, airspace, radio calls, takeoffs, landings and navigation.
Eligibility
Airplane certificate applicants must be at least 17. Discuss student pilot and medical eligibility before solo.
Training benefits
Develop the skills to fly for personal travel and pursue additional ratings.
Training EnquiryBook a Discovery Flight

Instrument Rating

Course overview
Practice instrument procedures in a structured ground and flight syllabus.
What you’ll learn
Instrument scan, IFR planning, holds, approaches and missed approaches.
Eligibility
Hold at least a private pilot certificate with the appropriate airplane rating, or apply for both together. Review required experience with an instructor.
Training benefits
Build proficiency for flying under instrument flight rules.
Training EnquiryBook a Discovery Flight

Commercial Pilot Certificate

Course overview
Refine aircraft control and judgment for commercial pilot training.
What you’ll learn
Commercial maneuvers, precise energy management and cross-country planning.
Eligibility
Applicants must be at least 18 and hold at least a private pilot certificate or qualify through the applicable military pathway. Confirm experience and endorsements.
Training benefits
Prepare to exercise commercial privileges where the applicable operating rules allow.
Training EnquiryBook a Discovery Flight

Certificated Flight Instructor (CFI)

Course overview
Develop the ground and in-flight teaching skills needed to instruct other pilots.
What you’ll learn
Lesson design, demonstration, student evaluation and instructional risk management.
Eligibility
Applicants must be at least 18 and hold a commercial or airline transport pilot certificate with appropriate ratings. Confirm instrument qualifications and endorsements.
Training benefits
Prepare to teach and help students progress toward their own certificates.
Training EnquiryBook a Discovery Flight

Certified Flight Instructor Instrument (CFII)

Course overview
Develop the teaching skills to guide pilots through instrument training.
What you’ll learn
Instrument lesson planning, IFR procedures, demonstrations and student evaluation.
Eligibility
Discuss your existing pilot and instructor certificates, instrument qualifications and endorsements with the team.
Training benefits
Prepare to help pilots develop proficiency and sound judgment when flying by reference to instruments.

Multi-engine training

Multi-Engine Commercial Training

Course overview
Transition to twin-engine operations with instruction in aircraft systems and performance, and prepare for commercial multi-engine flying. Multi-engine add-on training is also available.
What you’ll learn
Asymmetric thrust, engine-out procedures, cockpit flows and performance planning.
Eligibility
For an add-on, bring your existing pilot certificate. The instructor will review your category, class and checkride requirements.
Training benefits
Expand the aircraft classes you can fly and build twin-engine proficiency.
Training EnquiryBook a Discovery Flight

Multi-Engine Instructor Training

Course overview
Build the instructional skills to teach twin-engine operations.
What you’ll learn
Explain multi-engine systems, demonstrate procedures and guide another pilot.
Eligibility
Start with a review of your certificates, ratings and flight experience with an instructor.
Training benefits
Develop a training plan for teaching in the Beechcraft Travel Air.

Specialty Training

Course overview
Tailor training to your aircraft transition, proficiency or local flying goals.
What you’ll learn
Ask about G1000 transition, L.A. airspace, Catalina and high-elevation checkouts, flight reviews and instrument proficiency checks.
Eligibility
Prerequisites depend on the activity. The team can review your certificate, recent experience and aircraft needs.
Training benefits
Target the skills and procedures you want to practice with an instructor.
