PLAN WITH CONFIDENCE

Know before
you go.

Who is MEI training for?

MEI training is for pilots working toward teaching in multi-engine airplanes. Your starting requirements depend on your pilot and instructor qualifications. Share your certificates, ratings and multi-engine time so an instructor can review the right path.

What will I work on during MEI training?

Training covers how to explain aircraft systems, plan lessons, demonstrate procedures and evaluate a student. Discuss performance planning, engine-out instruction and practical-test preparation with your instructor.

Can I train if I’m coming from outside Los Angeles?

Contact the school with your travel dates and training goals before making arrangements. Aircraft, instructor, and examiner availability must be confirmed directly; an accelerated finish is not promised.

Is there a guaranteed completion time or fixed price?

No. A realistic estimate depends on prior experience, proficiency, preparation, scheduling, weather, aircraft availability, and any practical-test arrangements. Request current details from the school.

What happens after I request a consultation?

Submit your inquiry and the training team can discuss a plan and availability with you. If automatic delivery is unavailable, you can review and send an email instead. Submitting an inquiry does not automatically confirm a booking.
