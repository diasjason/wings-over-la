Ask a Training Question
What is MEI training?

MEI means Multi-Engine Instructor. It is for pilots working toward teaching in twin-engine aircraft. Share your pilot and instructor certificates, ratings and multi-engine time so the team can review your starting point.

Can I try flying without any experience?

Yes. Ask about a discovery flight: an introductory lesson with an instructor. Before arranging it, confirm the duration, price, what to bring, guest limits and weather policy with the school.

How do I start learning to fly?

Contact the team about Private Pilot training. You’ll discuss ground study, flight lessons, eligibility and a schedule that works for you. A discovery flight is a useful first step if you’re still deciding.

How much does training cost, and how long does it take?

It depends on your starting experience, lesson frequency and progress. Ask for a current estimate covering aircraft, instruction and any additional fees. Weather and availability can also affect your schedule.

What do I need to rent an aircraft?

Tell the team your certificates, ratings, recent flight experience and preferred aircraft. Ask about the aircraft checkout, insurance, current hourly rate and reservation terms. Rental approval and availability must be confirmed.

Where is Wings Over L.A. based?

Find us at 7900 Balboa Blvd., Suite J1, Van Nuys, CA 91406, at Van Nuys Airport. Call (818) 922-2449 before visiting for directions and airport access instructions.
