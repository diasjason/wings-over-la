FOR JESS
1. Download and extract/unzip the ENTIRE ZIP file first.
   On Windows: right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
   On Mac: double-click the ZIP to extract it.
2. Open the extracted Wings-Over-LA-Local-Preview folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the homepage in Chrome, Edge or Safari.
4. Use the navigation to browse all 13 pages. Keep all files and folders together.
